The Illuminator's Path

1726 years ago Saint Gregory walked this way

✦

We retrace his route — from the Cathedral in Yerevan to Lake Sevan, to where he founded a monastery and raised a cross. Every day of the procession is a step of the spiritual journey.

301

Saint Gregory the Illuminator converts King Trdat III. Armenia becomes the first Christian nation in the world.

4th c.

Gregory founds a monastery on Sevan island — the future Sevanavank. The first churches of the route are built.

7th c.

The monasteries of Karenis and Ptghnavank are raised — the churches where the molebens of the march will be served.

20th c.

The churches are devastated and stand empty. Services have not been celebrated in them for decades.

2027

We walk this path anew — with the cross and prayer, bringing worship back to the ancient walls.

Journey's finale · Day 5

Sevanavank, founded by Gregory the Illuminator in the 4th century

Sevan Peninsula · 4th c. / 9th c.

According to tradition, the first church of the future monastery on the rock was founded by Gregory the Illuminator himself in the 4th century, when he planted a great cross there, while the ensemble that has come down to us is the gift of Princess Mariam: in 874 she built two churches here, Surb Arakelots (Holy Apostles) and Surb Astvatsatsin (Mother of God). For centuries the monastery stood on an island, and only in the 20th century, when the level of the lake fell, did the rock join the shore.

Relics of the procession

The relics that walk with us

✦

These are not relics from museum cases but living sanctities that travel with the column the whole way and are opened for veneration at every stop.

The Wooden Cross of the Procession

Blessed by being touched to the reliquary containing a fragment of the Life-Giving Tree in Etchmiadzin Cathedral. This is the principal holy object: it is carried at the head of the column the whole way, and at Sevanavank it is planted beside the church, just as Saint Gregory the Illuminator planted the cross at Sevan.

For pilgrims

What to bring and how it all works

✦

We have made everything so that you travel light, focused on prayer. The whole camp life — from hot meals and water supply to tent nights, luggage transport and medical care — is fully provided by the organizers.

Gear

  • ✦Daypack: a light backpack that you carry yourself at all times, with everything you need for one day on the road.
  • ✦In your backpack: water, a raincoat, a power bank, a personal first-aid kit, band-aids, a light jacket for the evening, a pack of wet wipes, a foam sit pad, sunscreen and a pocket snack.
  • ✦Clothing for the march: comfortable, well broken-in shoes, plus a hat or headscarf for protection from the harsh July sun.
